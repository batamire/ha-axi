### Hygiene (standing rule)

This repo becomes public at publish time. NEVER write secrets, tokens, LAN or
tailnet IPs, hostnames, or other home-identifying details into issues,
comments, docs, fixtures, or test data. Use redacted placeholders
(`https://hass.example`) and synthetic entity ids (`light.example`).
